# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Reviewkit is a Chrome extension that runs in your browser. This policy explains what the extension accesses and what happens to that information.

## Data we do not collect

Reviewkit does not create accounts, does not run its own servers for review storage, and does not sell your data. Reviews fetched with the extension are not uploaded to Reviewkit.

## What the extension accesses

- Open browser tabs, so it can detect Google Play Store app pages and package names you already have open.
- Public Google Play review pages, to download reviews you asked it to extract.
- Your device’s downloads, so it can save the Excel file locally.

## Where data stays

Review data is processed in your browser and saved as an Excel file on your computer. We do not receive a copy of that file.

## Third parties

When the extension fetches reviews, it talks to Google Play. Google’s own privacy terms apply to that service. Reviewkit is not affiliated with Google.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
