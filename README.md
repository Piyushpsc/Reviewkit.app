# Reviewkit

Reviewkit is a Chrome extension for collecting Google Play reviews. Add apps from open Play Store tabs or by package name, choose language, country, sort, and star rating, then extract. Reviews are fetched in batches of 1,000 and saved on your computer as Excel. Reviewkit does not upload your exports to a server.
